# Chai & Co Hospitality — Employee Handbook (v2.1)

This handbook applies to all staff at Brew Lane Café, Tandoor Express and Cloud Kitchen HSR. It is a simplified sample policy for the ShiftPilot demo and is not legal advice.

## 1. Working Hours

### 1.1 Shifts
Standard shifts are 8 hours: opening (7:00–15:00), mid (11:00–19:00) and closing (15:00–23:00). Rosters are published every Thursday for the following week. Staff may not work more than 6 shifts in a week without written approval from the outlet manager.

### 1.2 Clocking in and out
Every shift must be clocked in and out on the ShiftPilot app at the outlet. Clocking in more than 10 minutes after the shift start is recorded as late. If a staff member forgets to clock out, they are paid until the scheduled end of the shift, and the manager must correct the record within 3 days.

### 1.3 Breaks
Staff working more than 5 hours receive a 30-minute paid meal break. Breaks are not clocked separately.

### 1.4 Rest between shifts
There must be at least 10 hours of rest between the end of one shift and the start of the next. Managers must not schedule a closing shift followed by an opening shift.

## 2. Overtime

### 2.1 When overtime applies
Overtime applies to hours worked beyond 9 hours in a day, or beyond 48 regular hours in a week (Monday to Sunday). Daily overtime is counted first; weekly overtime is then counted on the remaining regular hours.

### 2.2 Overtime rate
Overtime is paid at twice (2x) the employee's ordinary hourly rate for the day on which it was worked.

### 2.3 Approval and limits
Overtime must be approved by the outlet manager. No one may work more than 12 hours in a single day; days above 12 hours are reviewed by the owner.

## 3. Pay

### 3.1 Pay periods
Pay is calculated weekly, Monday to Sunday, and paid on the following Wednesday by bank transfer.

### 3.2 Rate changes
When an hourly rate changes, the new rate applies from its effective date. Days before the change in the same week are paid at the old rate.

### 3.3 Payslips
Every employee can see their own payslip in ShiftPilot. Staff cannot see other employees' pay. Managers can see pay for their own outlet only.

## 4. Tips

### 4.1 Collection
All card and cash tips are pooled per outlet per day. Tips are not part of wages and are paid in full to staff.

### 4.2 Distribution
At Brew Lane Café, tips are shared in proportion to the hours each person worked that day. At Tandoor Express, tips are shared equally among everyone who worked that day. Cloud Kitchen HSR does not collect tips.

### 4.3 Disputes
Any difference between tips collected and tips allocated must be investigated by the manager within 7 days and corrected in the next pay run.

## 5. Deductions

### 5.1 Allowed deductions
Only these deductions may be made: staff meals (as agreed in writing), salary advance recovery, uniform replacement, damage caused by proven negligence, and statutory deductions (PF, ESI, TDS) where applicable.

### 5.2 Limits and approval
Managers may approve a deduction of up to ₹2,000. Any single deduction above ₹2,000 needs approval from the owner. Total deductions in a week must not exceed 50% of that week's gross pay.

### 5.3 Uniforms
The first uniform set is free. Replacement aprons and caps cost ₹240 per set and are deducted from the next pay.

## 6. Leave

### 6.1 Weekly off
Every employee gets at least one full day off each week.

### 6.2 Sick leave
Staff get 7 days of paid sick leave per year. Inform your manager before your shift starts.

### 6.3 Shift swaps
Staff may swap shifts with a colleague of the same role. Swaps must be requested at least 24 hours in advance and approved by the manager.

## 7. Conduct and Safety

### 7.1 Food safety
Fridge temperatures must be recorded at opening and closing. Fridges must stay between 1°C and 5°C.

### 7.2 Opening and closing checklists
The opening and closing checklists must be completed every day in ShiftPilot. Incomplete closing checklists are escalated to the manager the next morning.
